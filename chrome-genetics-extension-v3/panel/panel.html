Theory of the Program
EXTRACT: Samples a live website into typed codons.

GENOME: Turns those codons into an editable prompt genotype with EXON / INTRON control.

FIELD: Applies the active genotype to a target task and renders the completion artifact.

LINEAGE / BREED: Stores genomes, selects parents, crosses codons, and expresses offspring.
